HAPPY BIRTHDAY - MY KUCHUU PUCHUU
=================================
Files in this zip:
  index.html        the birthday page
  add-my-song.html  small helper that puts YOUR song inside the birthday page

HOW TO USE YOUR OWN SONG AS BACKGROUND MUSIC (Teri Ore mp3)
 1. Extract (unzip) the zip.
 2. Open add-my-song.html in Chrome.
 3. Step 1: choose index.html.  Step 2: choose your mp3.  Tap "Make my birthday page".
 4. It downloads  birthday-with-my-song.html  - open THAT file in Chrome.
    Your song is now inside the page, so it plays on any phone you send it to.
    (Everything happens on your own device - nothing is uploaded.)

HOW THE MUSIC WORKS
 - Tap "Start your surprise": your background song starts automatically.
 - It plays on every screen except the song screen.
 - On the song screen it stops and the song plays; tap "Next surprise" and the song
   stops and your background song starts again automatically.
 - There are no music buttons or symbols on the page.

Quick alternative on one phone: open the page and tap the big "Happy Birthday" title
3 times quickly to choose a song from that phone (remembered on that phone).
